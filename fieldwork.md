
## Real-World Problem Observed at Samrat Bazar

During my visit to Samrat Bazar, I learned about a stock-related incident in which some products were reportedly missing from the shop.

The interesting part was that the missing products were not identified immediately. The shortage became known only after a few days.

### Why This Problem Matters

This incident helped me understand that maintaining product records is not enough. Shopkeepers also need a reliable way to compare their recorded stock with the actual physical quantity.

For example:

- The inventory system records 20 units of a product.
- The physical count shows only 17 units.
- The application should highlight a shortage of 3 units.
- The shopkeeper can then investigate recent sales, purchases, returns, damaged products, and other stock movements.

### How My Project Can Help

Karo Shopping aims to help shopkeepers:

1. Maintain product-wise inventory records.
2. Record sales and automatically update stock quantities.
3. Record incoming stock and manual adjustments.
4. Compare recorded quantities with physical stock counts.
5. Identify and investigate stock differences earlier.

The application cannot independently confirm theft. It helps identify discrepancies so that the shopkeeper can investigate them.
