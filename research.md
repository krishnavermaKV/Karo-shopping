
## 1. Problem Statement

Small retail shops manage many products, and maintaining accurate stock records can be difficult.

1. Shopkeepers need to track product purchases, sales, and available quantities.
2. When stock movements are not recorded correctly, the system quantity may differ from the physical quantity.
3. Missing products or stock shortages may not be noticed immediately.
4. If stock is checked only occasionally, the shopkeeper may discover a shortage several days after it happens.
5. I want to build an inventory management tool that helps shopkeepers maintain product records and identify stock discrepancies earlier.

## 2. Real-World Problem That Motivated This Project

During my visit to Samrat Bazar, I learned about a reported incident in which some products went missing from the shop. The shortage was reportedly discovered only after a few days.

This made me think about how useful an inventory management application could be.

If product purchases, sales, and other stock movements are recorded properly, the shopkeeper can maintain updated inventory records and compare them with physical stock.

The application can highlight differences between recorded and actual quantities, helping the shopkeeper investigate missing products, incorrect entries, or unrecorded transactions.
